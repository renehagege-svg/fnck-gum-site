The Opportunity

The Market Gap

The functional gummy market is exploding, yet no premium botanical brand dominates the DTC space. Most competitors use synthetic actives, hide behind proprietary blends, or target a mass-market audience with cheap packaging.

FNCK GUM occupies the whitespace: clinically dosed botanicals, transparent labeling, premium design, and a provocative brand that resonates with millennials and Gen Z wellness consumers.

The Model

DTC-first with a heavy subscription component. Four foundational SKUs with clear use cases. Low COGS at scale ($9/unit). High margins (70% gross). Low CAC through viral marketing and brand provocation.

The Team

René Hagege — Founder. Serial entrepreneur with exits in food & beverage (PCD) and digital (AIAA). Deep operational expertise in multi-location business management, supply chain, and brand building.
